System FAQ

Operational guidelines for AirPulse Hotspot gateway.

How does M-Pesa STK authentication function?

Tap any price pass, submit your phone number, and authorization is sent as an automated SIM toolkit PIN prompt. Once authorized, the gateway marks your MAC address as authenticated.

Does the session persist on Wi-Fi reconnect?

Yes. The RouterOS radius profile retains your session time until expiration. Rejoining the network does not prompt for repayment.

Device switching and voucher codes

Each transaction produces an alphanumeric "AP-" voucher backup code. Use "Voucher Code" on any secondary machine to transfer session rights.
